Bios

Short and long bio.

Akrem Ziani is a Montreal-based entrepreneur and the founder and CEO of Onzaris, management software for all of sport.

Akrem Ziani is a Montreal-based entrepreneur and the founder and CEO of Onzaris, management software for all of sport. Onzaris connects registrations, schedules, referees and officials, game sheets, results, payments and communication around every game, for clubs, leagues, tournaments, schools and federations, on the web and on iOS and Android. Before Onzaris, Akrem built websites for small businesses (Lazo Webs), ran Lazo Growth, a sales agency where he recruited and trained a 25-student cold-calling team that made more than 600 calls, and built Vocalype, a desktop voice assistant.
